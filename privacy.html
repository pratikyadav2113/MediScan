Privacy Policy for MediScan

At MediScan, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when using our services.

Information We Collect

MediScan may collect the following information:

  • Prescription images uploaded by users for medicine recognition.
  • Contact information voluntarily provided through our Contact Us page,
    such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and device
    information, to improve website performance.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Process uploaded prescription images using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
  • Identify medicine names from the prescription.
  • Improve the accuracy and performance of our recognition system.
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests.
  • Maintain and improve the overall functionality of MediScan.

Image Storage

  • Uploaded prescription images may be stored securely in
    our database for processing, system improvement, and
    future reference where applicable. Access to these images
    is restricted to authorized system processes and administrators.

Data Security

  • We implement appropriate security measures to help protect
    your uploaded data and personal information from unauthorized
    access, alteration, or disclosure. While we strive to use commercially
    acceptable methods to safeguard your information, no internet-based
    system can guarantee complete security.

Third-Party Services

  • MediScan may provide links to trusted online pharmacy websites
    to help users find medicines. These external websites have their
    own privacy policies, and MediScan is not responsible for their
    content, security, or data handling practices.

Cookies

  • MediScan may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website
    functionality, remember user preferences, and enhance the
    browsing experience.

User Responsibility

  • Users should upload clear and readable prescription images
    for the best OCR results. MediScan is intended to assist
    users in identifying medicines and should not replace the
    advice of qualified healthcare professionals.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
    Any changes will be posted on this page with the latest revision.

Contact Us

  • If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy
    or how your information is handled, please contact us using the
    phone number or email address provided on our Contact Us page.
