Frequently Asked Questions
FAQ

What is MediScan?

MediScan is a web platform that allows users to upload a prescription, identify the medicines listed, and easily find them from trusted online pharmacy sources.

Which prescription formats
are supported?

MediScan works with clear images of printed prescriptions in common formats such as JPG, JPEG, and PNG.

2

3

Is my prescription information
secure?

We value your privacy. Your prescription is handled responsibly, and protecting user data is one of our priorities.

Does MediScan sell
medicines?

No. MediScan does not sell medicines directly. It helps users identify medicines and provides links to trusted online pharmacies where they may be available.

4

5

Can MediScan read handwritten
prescriptions?

MediScan performs best with clear, printed prescriptions. Recognition accuracy for handwritten prescriptions may vary depending on handwriting quality.

Why couldn't MediScan identify
all the medicines?

Poor image quality, blurred text, unusual prescription formats, or difficult handwriting can affect recognition accuracy. Uploading a clearer image usually improves the results.

6

7

Is MediScan free to
use?

Yes, MediScan is free to use for identifying medicines from prescriptions. However, purchasing medicines from online pharmacies may involve costs.

How does MediScan work?

MediScan uses advanced image recognition technology to analyze the uploaded prescription. It identifies the medicines listed and provides users with information on where to purchase them from reliable online pharmacies.
