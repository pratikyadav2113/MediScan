ABOUT US

MediScan

At MediScan, we believe that accessing medicine information should be simple and reliable. Our platform helps users scan prescriptions, identify medicines, and compare prices from multiple pharmacies—all in one place. We are committed to making healthcare smarter, faster, and more affordable.

Why Choose MediScan

At MediScan, we make understanding your prescription simple and convenient. Instead of manually searching for every medicine, our platform scans your prescription, identifies the medicines, and helps you find them from trusted online pharmacies. We focus on saving your time, reducing confusion caused by difficult-to-read prescriptions, and making healthcare information more accessible.

Fast Prescription Scanning

Upload your prescription and let MediScan quickly identify the medicines listed.

Accurate Medicine Recognition

Compare Pharmacy Options

Find medicines from multiple trusted pharmacy websites in one place, making it easier to compare availability and pricing.

Secure & Private

Easy-to-Use Interface

Save Time
